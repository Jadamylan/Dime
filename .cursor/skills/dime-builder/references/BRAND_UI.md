# Dime — Brand and UI Reference

## Brand

**Name:** Dime  
**Tagline:** Tell me in your language.

Use the approved Dime continuous-line logo in `../assets/Dime_Approved_Logo.png`.

Do not redraw, regenerate, restyle, or replace the approved logo unless explicitly asked.

## Product statement

**Dime turns iPhone Duo into the person between two people who don't speak the same language.**

Supporting thesis:

**Two people. Two languages. One shared understanding.**

## Palette

- Warm white background: `#FAFAF7`
- Primary ink: `#111111`
- Dime lime: `#CFF75E`
- Soft surface: `#F0F0EA`
- Clarification / caution gold: `#F4C65B`

Use dark text on lime. Do not use white text on Dime lime.

## Typography

### Actual app
Use Apple system typography through SwiftUI `.system()` / SF Pro.

Prefer semantic text styles:
- title / largeTitle for primary headings
- headline for cards and state labels
- body for conversation content
- callout / footnote for secondary explanatory text

Support Dynamic Type.

### Static visual references
The included PNGs may use Inter as a mockup fallback. Do not treat that as a requirement for the shipped app.

## Visual tone

- Calm
- Human
- Clear
- Minimal
- High contrast
- Accessible
- Not overly clinical
- No gradients
- No glassmorphism
- No decorative medical imagery
- No dense dashboards

## Approved screen flow

1. Topic selection
2. Healthcare context
3. Student speaks / clinician sees translation
4. Clinician responds / student sees translation
5. Clarification state
6. Fully-open bilingual recap

## Topic selection

Show five topics:
- Healthcare — selected for the demo
- Travel
- Housing
- Education
- Work

## Demo scenario

Santiago, Chile.

An English-speaking college student studying abroad is about 8 weeks pregnant. She can manage everyday Spanish but needs help explaining symptoms and understanding prenatal-care communication.

## Emoji rule

If people emoji appear in the demo copy or presentation, use chocolate-brown skin tone:
- Student: 👩🏾‍🎓
- Clinician: 🧑🏾‍⚕️
